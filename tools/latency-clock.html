Procedure

  1. Show this clock on a screen in front of the client camera, so it goes through VTX → VRX → relay → HM30 → GCS.
  2. Put the GCS screen with the relayed video next to this clock.
  3. Film both together at 240 fps. In single frames of the recording, read this clock and the clock in the GCS video.
  4. Latency sample = this clock − clock in the GCS video (ms). Record 20 samples; report the mean and worst case (relay design, test 2).

The display's own frame interval (above) limits the resolution of one reading.
