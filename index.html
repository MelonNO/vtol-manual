Telemetry

Analyse a flight-controller DataFlash log or a GCS session log, and open the latency test clock.

DataFlash log (.bin)

The .bin log from the flight controller SD card. Richer and more complete than .tlog — extracts battery, altitude, airspeed, GPS, flight modes, and status messages.

GCS session log (.jsonl)

A session-*.jsonl file written by the GCS companion app (%APPDATA%\vtol-gcs\logs): RC round-trip time, uplink loss, client link quality, client battery, bridge-state timeline and alarms.

Latency test clock

Relay-design test 2 (glass-to-glass video): film this clock and the GCS screen together at 240 fps, 20 samples, record the mean and worst case.

Maintenance
How maintenance is counted

Intervals from the manual's Maintenance chapter: carrier every 5, 10 and 25 flights or monthly; client drone every 5 flights. A flight is one battery-log entry (carrier packs for the carrier, the client's pack for a client). DUE = do it before the next flight; OVERDUE = flown past the interval.
