⚠ EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
Memorise the immediate actions. Perform them without reference. When stable, open the clean-up section.
RC Link Loss — 915MHz (Carrier Control)
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
WAIT — do not touch TX
2
1.5s: FBWA AUTO — monitor MP
3
5s: RTL AUTO — 120m AGL
4
GCS STILL LIVE: MP mode commands
5
NO TX POWER-CYCLE in flight
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
Confirm RTL altitude in MP — should show 120m AGL before descent begins.
2
Monitor airspeed — must stay above 11 m/s. Use MP GCS for mode commands if needed.
3
Monitor battery — extended RTL draws higher current during VTOL descent phase.
4
If link recovers: assess battery and position before resuming AUTO mission.
5
After landing: inspect TX/RX antennas, check bind, log the incident.
Relay Link Loss — 2.4GHz (FPV Sub-Drone)
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
FPV PILOT: HANDS OFF — now
2
CARRIER: HOLD LOITER
3
GPS RESCUE fires in 2.5s — wait
4
DO NOT touch FPV TX sticks
5
WATCH OSD: need 8+ GPS sats
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
GPS Rescue: climbs 30m, cruises home at 100m AGL / 7.5 m/s, motor cut at 4m.
2
Monitor using SIYI carrier camera if FPV is in view.
3
Keep carrier loitering until FPV lands — sub-drone may be returning toward carrier position.
4
Confirm FPV disarmed before approach on ground.
5
Check relay bind was completed pre-flight. Inspect carrier Nomad relay TX antenna.
Video Loss — 5.8GHz or SIYI Chain
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
FPV PILOT: ZERO INPUTS — hover
2
CARRIER: DESCEND — reduce range
3
WAIT 10 SECONDS
4
STILL LOST: FPV return home
5
CARRIER: independent recovery
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
Check CVBS→HDMI converter — connector, power light on, no heat damage.
2
Check SIYI HDMI→IP encoder — connected to SIYI switch, LED status normal.
3
Verify FPV VTX channel: Raceband R5 / 5806 MHz.
4
Check 5.8G VRX antenna orientation on carrier.
5
Restart GStreamer and MPV stream. Verify video in MPV.net before FPV re-arms.
Transition Failure — VTOL → FW
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
QHOVER — switch now
2
GAIN ALTITUDE — full VTOL power
3
HOLD — assess before any retry
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
Check pitot — airspeed reading in MP? Zero = blocked/disconnected. Do not retry without airspeed data.
2
Check tilt servos — switch to FBWB and observe in MP. Do front rotors tilt forward?
3
Check battery — sufficient for another transition attempt plus VTOL recovery?
4
Check wind — clear corridor ahead? Crosswind/tailwind can prevent successful transition.
5
If retrying: climb to 60m+ in QHOVER first. Clear 300m corridor ahead. Monitor airspeed immediately on AUTO.
6
Log failure phase: no airspeed build in 3s vs pitch-down upset. Note battery voltage.
Battery Critical — Carrier
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
23.0V → RTL NOW — left button
2
22.5V → RTL MUST BE ACTIVE
3
22.2V → AUTOLAND HERE — not home
4
CLEAR AREA BELOW
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
Log landing voltage in battery log — note start voltage and any anomalies.
2
Inspect battery for puffing, heat, smell. If any present — quarantine, do not charge.
3
Allow pack to cool 30 minutes minimum before charging.
4
Review: was RTL initiated on time? Use battery log analysis to check sag vs previous flights.
5
Set BATT_LOW_VOLT using the battery log recommendation in the Tools page.
GCS Link Loss (SIYI HM30)
IMMEDIATE — FROM MEMORY
1
SWITCH — 915MHz Nomad TX now
2
CONFIRM carrier responds
3
AUTO: GCS failsafe → RTL fires
4
OTHER: Nomad switch + left button
CLEAN-UP · NOT FOR MEMORY
1
Check ethernet cable — mast to laptop. Reseat both ends.
2
Check HM30 ground unit LED — blue = linked. If not: check mast battery and HM30 power.
3
Check mast battery voltage — below 21.0V may cause HM30 brownout.
4
Restart MP UDPCI: IP 192.168.144.12 / Port 19856.
5
Do not re-enter AUTO until GCS telemetry stable for 30+ seconds.

Immediate Post-Landing
1
Disarm carrier

Confirm disarmed in Mission Planner. All motors stopped. Do not approach until disarmed.

2
Disarm FPV sub-drone

Co-pilot confirms FPV disarmed. Props stopped.

3
Disconnect carrier battery

Disconnect 6S pack. All systems power down. Record landing voltage.

4
Disconnect FPV battery

Record FPV landing voltage.

Ground Station Teardown
1
Check mast battery voltage

Disconnect and measure 6S 3000mAh mast battery. Below 21.0V: charge immediately, do not store discharged.

2
Power down HM30 ground unit

Disconnect ethernet. Remove mast battery safely.

3
Lower and secure mast

Confirm HM30 and cabling secured before transport.

4
Close Mission Planner

Save log if required.

Inspection
1
Carrier airframe

Inspect tilt motor mounts, wing joins, prop blades, connectors. Check for heat or smell from ESCs/motors.

2
Carrier battery

Check for puffing, heat, damage. Store at 22.8V (3.8V/cell) if not flying within 48h. Never store discharged — BATT_CRT voltage (22.2V) is not a storage target.

3
Video chain

Check CVBS→HDMI and SIYI HDMI→IP for heat. Inspect VRX antenna.

4
FPV sub-drone

Inspect props, motors, frame.
